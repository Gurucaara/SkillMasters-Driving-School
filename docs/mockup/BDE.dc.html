BDE QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is Skillmasters MTO-approved for BDE?

Yes. Skillmasters Driving School Ltd. is an MTO-licensed BDE course provider (licence #L4H7).

Can I start BDE before getting my G1?

You can start online learning, but you need a valid G1 before any in-car lessons.

How soon can I take my G2 test?

BDE graduates can take the G2 road test after 8 months with a G1, instead of 12.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Eligible students who complete all parts have their completion reported according to MTO requirements.
