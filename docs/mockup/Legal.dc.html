Privacy, terms & refunds

Plain-language policies so you know exactly how we work.

Who we are

Skillmasters Driving School Ltd. ("we"), MTO BDE Course Provider Licence #L4H7, provides driver education in London, Ontario and nearby areas. Questions about privacy: info@skillmastersdrivingschool.ca.

What we collect

Your name, email, phone number, pickup area, licence stage, and the lessons you book. For BDE students, MTO reporting requires your driver’s licence number, date of birth and licence expiry date. We collect these through a secure form after payment, never by email or text.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe’s own privacy policy applies to payment data.

Why we use it

To schedule lessons, contact you, send receipts and reminders, report BDE completion to the MTO, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep records for at least 6 years, as required for tax and MTO reporting, then delete them securely.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information (where the law allows) by emailing us. We follow Canada’s privacy law (PIPEDA).

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics with no advertising cookies.

Eligibility

In-car lessons require a valid Ontario G1 or G2 licence. Students must be 16 or older.

Bookings

Lessons are confirmed once paid in full.

Safety

Your instructor may end or reschedule a lesson for safety reasons, including severe weather or if a student appears impaired.

Package validity

Packages are valid for 12 months from purchase.

Prices

All prices are in CAD and subject to 13% HST. Prices may change, but paid packages keep their price.

Rescheduling

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson, by text, call, WhatsApp or email.

Late cancellations & no-shows

Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice, or missed lessons, are charged a $50 fee.

Refunds

If you cancel within 15 days of registering, you receive a 50% refund. After 15 days, payments are non-refundable.
