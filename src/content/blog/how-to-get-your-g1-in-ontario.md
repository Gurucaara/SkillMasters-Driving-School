Your G1 is the first step to driving in Ontario. It's a learner's licence: you can practise on the road, but only with an experienced driver beside you. Here's how to get one, without any surprises at DriveTest.

## Who can apply

You need to be **at least 16 years old**. There's no driving involved yet: the G1 is earned with two short tests at a DriveTest centre.

## Step 1: Study the Official Driver's Handbook

The knowledge test is based on the Ministry of Transportation's *Official MTO Driver's Handbook*. It's available free online at [ontario.ca](https://www.ontario.ca/document/official-mto-drivers-handbook). Focus on two areas:

- **Road signs:** shapes, colours and what each sign tells you to do.
- **Rules of the road:** right-of-way, speed limits, school buses, emergency vehicles, and the rules for new drivers.

Most students need a couple of weeks of short daily study. Read a chapter, then test yourself.

> **Try it now:** our [free G1 practice test](/g1-practice-test) has real-style questions on signs and rules, with the reason behind every answer.

## Step 2: Book or visit DriveTest

G1 tests are done at DriveTest centres. In our area, that's the London DriveTest centre. Check current hours, fees and whether you need to book on [drivetest.ca](https://www.drivetest.ca) before you go, as these change.

## Step 3: Bring the right documents

Bring **original** identification (not photocopies) that shows your legal name, date of birth and signature, for example a passport or a Canadian citizenship or permanent resident card. DriveTest lists exactly which documents are accepted, so check before your visit.

If you wear glasses or contact lenses, bring them for the vision test.

## Step 4: Pass the vision test

A quick eye test. If you need glasses to pass, your licence will say you must wear them while driving.

## Step 5: Pass the knowledge test

A multiple-choice test on signs and rules. If you don't pass, don't worry: you can study and try again.

## You have your G1. Now what?

With a G1 you **must**:

- Have **zero** alcohol in your blood (and no cannabis or other drugs).
- Drive with a fully licensed driver beside you who has **at least 4 years** of driving experience.
- Make sure everyone in the car wears a seat belt.
- Stay off 400-series highways and high-speed expressways, unless your accompanying driver is a licensed driving instructor.
- Not drive between **midnight and 5 a.m.**

You must hold your G1 for **12 months** before taking the G2 road test, or **8 months** if you complete an MTO-approved Beginner Driver Education (BDE) course.
