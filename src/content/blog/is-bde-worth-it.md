"Do I really need a driving school, or can my parents teach me?" It's the question I hear most. Here's an honest look at what a Beginner Driver Education (BDE) course gives you, so you can decide.

## What BDE is

BDE is a driver education course approved by Ontario's Ministry of Transportation (MTO). Only licensed course providers can offer it. Skillmasters is one: our MTO BDE Course Provider Licence is **#L4H7**.

An MTO-approved course includes at least **40 hours**:

- **20 hours** of classroom or online learning: rules, hazards, defensive driving.
- **10 hours** of in-car training with a licensed instructor.
- **10 hours** of flexible "home-link" activities you complete at your own pace.

When you finish, your completion is reported to the MTO.

## Benefit 1: An earlier road test

Normally you must hold your G1 for **12 months** before you can take your G2 road test. Complete an approved BDE course and that drops to **8 months**. That's four months sooner on the road on your own.

## Benefit 2: Possible insurance savings

Many insurance companies offer a discount to new drivers who complete an approved BDE course. Discounts vary by company, so ask your insurer what they offer before you choose a course. For a new driver, insurance is often the biggest yearly cost of driving, so even a small percentage can add up.

## Benefit 3: Skills that practice alone doesn't build

Practising with family is valuable, and I encourage it. But an instructor brings things a parent usually can't:

- **A dual-brake car.** I can stop the car if something goes wrong, so you can learn calmly instead of nervously.
- **A plan for every lesson.** Each session builds on the last: from parking lots to intersections, lane changes and busy roads.
- **Up-to-date test knowledge.** I know what examiners look for, and the habits that cost students points.
- **No bad habits passed on.** Experienced drivers often pick up shortcuts (rolling stops, skipped shoulder checks) that fail road tests.

## When BDE might not be for you

If you're not in a hurry to take your G2, already have a lot of supervised practice, and your insurer offers no discount, private lessons before your test may be enough. Our [G2 & G lessons](/lessons) are built for exactly that.
