The G2 road test checks one thing: can you drive safely on your own on city roads? It isn't about being perfect. It's about being consistent, careful and in control. Use this checklist in the weeks before your test.

## Before test day

- [ ] You've held your G1 for **12 months**, or **8 months** with an approved BDE course.
- [ ] Your test is booked on [drivetest.ca](https://www.drivetest.ca). London tests get busy, so book early.
- [ ] You've practised in **all kinds of conditions**: rain, busy times, school zones, residential streets and multi-lane roads.
- [ ] You can do a **parallel park**, a **three-point turn** and a **roadside stop** calmly, without rushing.
- [ ] You've had at least one **mock test** with feedback from someone who knows what examiners look for.

## What examiners watch for

These are the habits that make up most of your score:

- **Observation.** Mirror checks before slowing, turning or changing lanes, and a **shoulder check** before every lane change and pulling away from the curb. Exaggerate your head movement so the examiner sees it.
- **Full stops.** Stop completely behind the line at every stop sign. A "rolling stop" is one of the most common reasons students fail.
- **Speed control.** Stay at the limit, not over it and not far under it. Watch for **school zones** and changing limits.
- **Signals.** Signal early, and make sure your signal turns off after you turn.
- **Lane position.** Stay centred in your lane, and turn into the correct lane.
- **Right-of-way.** Know who goes first at four-way stops, and yield to pedestrians at crossings.
- **Smoothness.** Gentle braking and steering show you're in control.

## Common mistakes that fail students

1. Rolling through a stop sign.
2. Missing a shoulder check on a lane change.
3. Speeding, especially in a school zone or when a limit drops.
4. Hesitating too long at an intersection when it's clearly your turn.
5. Hitting or climbing the curb while parking.
6. Not yielding to a pedestrian or cyclist.

## On the day

- [ ] Arrive **early**, with your G1 licence and your glasses if you need them.
- [ ] Bring a car that's in good working order, with valid insurance and ownership. Lights, signals, horn and brakes must all work, or the test can be cancelled.
- [ ] Get a **warm-up drive** before the test. Ten minutes behind the wheel settles nerves more than anything.
- [ ] Listen to each instruction. If you're not sure, ask the examiner to repeat it. That's allowed.
