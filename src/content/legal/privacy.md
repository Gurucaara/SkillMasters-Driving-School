## Who we are

Skillmasters Driving School Ltd. ("we"), MTO BDE Course Provider Licence #L4H7, provides driver education in London, Ontario and nearby areas. Questions about privacy: [info@skillmastersdrivingschool.ca](mailto:info@skillmastersdrivingschool.ca).

## What we collect

- **When you book:** your name, email, phone number, licence stage (G1 or G2), pickup area, preferred start date and time, and the package you buy.
- **When you send a message:** your name, phone number, email (if you give it), preferred contact method, pickup area, language and your message.
- **For BDE students:** MTO reporting requires your driver's licence number, date of birth and licence expiry date. We collect these through a secure form after payment, never by email, text or on this website's public forms.

## Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to payment data.

## Why we use it

To schedule lessons, contact you, send receipts and reminders, report BDE completion to the MTO, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your information.

## Who helps us

We use a few trusted service providers to run this website and our bookings: Stripe (payments), Cloudflare (website hosting and spam protection), and email and scheduling services. They only use your information to provide their service to us. Some of them store or process data outside Canada, for example in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws.

## How long we keep it

We keep records for at least 6 years, as required for tax and MTO reporting, then delete them securely.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information (where the law allows) by emailing us. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA).

## Cookies

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it.
