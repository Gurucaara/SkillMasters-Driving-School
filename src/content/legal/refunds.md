## Rescheduling

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule a lesson, by text, call, WhatsApp or email.

## Late cancellations & no-shows

Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed lessons, are charged a $50 fee.

## Refunds

If you cancel within 15 days of registering, you receive a 50% refund. After 15 days, payments are non-refundable.
